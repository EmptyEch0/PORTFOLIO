# Serea — Unified AI Document Workspace & Local LLM Suite

<div className="text-zinc-400 text-sm mb-8 font-serif">
  Cross-Platform Desktop & Mobile App • Flutter & Dart • On-Device LLM Brain (Ollama/GGUF) • Privacy-First RAG
</div>

<div className="space-y-8 text-[15.5px] text-zinc-300 leading-relaxed font-serif">

<p>
  <strong className="text-zinc-100 font-medium">Serea</strong> is a cross-platform, all-in-one AI document workspace built with Flutter for both <strong className="text-zinc-100">Laptop (Windows / macOS / Linux)</strong> and <strong className="text-zinc-100">Mobile (Android / iOS)</strong>. It unifies the document manipulation capabilities of <em>iLovePDF</em>, the conversational intelligence of <em>ChatGPT</em>, the knowledge synthesis of <em>NotebookLM</em>, and modern OCR into a single private ecosystem powered by an embedded <strong className="text-zinc-100">on-device LLM brain</strong>.
</p>

<h2 className="font-cormorant text-2xl text-zinc-100 font-semibold pt-4 mb-2">1. Core Philosophy: Why Serea?</h2>

<p>
  Students, researchers, and developers constantly juggle fragmented tools: one website to merge or compress PDFs, another browser tab for cloud AI chat, a third tool for OCR, and separate apps for study notes. This creates severe privacy concerns, subscription fatigue, and file disorganization.
</p>

<p>
  Serea solves this by placing a complete document workstation with an <strong className="text-zinc-100">offline AI brain directly on your device</strong>. Whether you are working on a flight without Wi-Fi, reviewing confidential institutional records, or studying dense textbooks on your phone, Serea processes everything locally with zero cloud dependencies.
</p>
